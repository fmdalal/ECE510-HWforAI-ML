Dominant projection uncertainty: memory bandwidth assumption
The accelerator lands at AI = 1,953 FLOP/byte, well into the compute-bound region, achieving the full projected peak of 49,150 GMAC/s. This is the expected outcome — the conformer kernel's high arithmetic intensity places it comfortably past the ridge point at 76.8 FLOP/byte on the accelerator roofline.
The dominant uncertainty in this projection is the assumed peak bandwidth of 640 GB/s across 10 UCIe links. This figure assumes all links are simultaneously saturated at full 512-bit width and 1 GHz, which requires perfect pipeline overlap across all 5 stages. In practice, UCIe credit-flow backpressure and flit serialization overhead will reduce effective bandwidth, shifting the ridge point rightward. If effective bandwidth drops below ~25 GB/s, the design would become memory-bound at AI = 1,953.
To convert to a measurement, the cocotb simulation would need to complete the full pipeline and measure actual bytes transferred versus cycles elapsed per UCIe link.
